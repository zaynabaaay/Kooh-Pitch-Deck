The Premise

KOOX is an unscripted variety and adventure series bringing together six older Somali men in Ottawa for games, competitions, and new experiences.

Each adventure brings out their personalities through playful rivalries, teamwork, and unexpected situations, as friendships develop along the way.

Market Overview

KOOX appeals to audiences interested in:

  • Unscripted comedy, variety shows, and reality competitions.
  • Personality-driven entertainment featuring humour, teamwork, and playful rivalries.
  • Somali and East African culture and representation.
  • Ottawa-based entertainment featuring recognizable locations, communities, and local businesses.
  • Older personalities taking on unexpected adventures, games, and challenges.
