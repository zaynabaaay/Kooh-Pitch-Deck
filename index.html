A new unscripted series

KOOX

Created by Zaynab Omer

THE PREMISE

KOOX is an unscripted variety and adventure series bringing together six Somali men aged 55 and older in Ottawa for games, competitions, and new experiences.

Each adventure brings out their personalities through playful rivalries, teamwork, and unexpected situations, as friendships develop along the way.

THE TONE

  • Lighthearted & Unpredictable

    Playful competition, friendly teasing, and spontaneous humour inspired by Running Man, 2 Days & 1 Night, and Taskmaster.

  • Fast-Paced & Relaxed

    Energetic challenges balanced with quieter moments where the men’s personalities and conversations come through.

  • Korean Variety-Show Editing

    Expressive captions, funny sound effects, reaction shots, replays, and upbeat music that add to the comedy.

SERIES FORMAT

Genre: Unscripted Variety / Comedy / Adventure

Season: 6 Episodes

Runtime: 40–45 Minutes per Episode

Format

Each adventure unfolds across two episodes, combining a series of games, competitions, and activities connected by a central theme.

The challenges create opportunities for spontaneous humour, friendly rivalries, teamwork, and unexpected moments as the cast navigates each experience.

Adventure 1

THE THRIFT STORE FASHION CHALLENGE

The six men take on the role of fashion stylists, competing to create the best outfits using clothing they find at a thrift store.

Their creations are revealed in a fashion show, where family and friends judge the final looks and determine the winner.

Adventure 2

THE RESTAURANT CHALLENGE

The six men spend several weeks learning how to cook and coming up with their own menu.

Once they’re ready, they open a restaurant for one night and invite the public to come and eat.

They’ll be responsible for everything from cooking the food to taking orders and serving customers. With a restaurant full of guests, they’ll have to work together to make the night a success.

Adventure 3

THE COMMERCIAL CHALLENGE

The six men take filmmaking lessons, learning how to plan, shoot, and direct a commercial.

They are then paired with a local business and given the challenge of creating their own advertisement.

From coming up with ideas to directing the shoot, the men take control of the creative process before presenting their finished commercial.

WHY KOOX?

  • An All-Somali Cast

    KOOX features six Somali men, bringing their humour, personalities, and cultural perspectives to a variety-show format.

  • An Entirely Older Cast (55+)

    Unlike its competitors, KOOX features a cast entirely aged 55 and older, placing older adults at the centre of the comedy and challenges.

  • An Ottawa-Based Series

    While its competitors are based elsewhere or travel across destinations, KOOX uses Ottawa’s neighbourhoods, businesses, and landmarks as the setting for its adventures.

TARGET AUDIENCE

  • Somali & East African Diaspora

    Viewers interested in seeing Somali culture, humour, and personalities represented in entertainment.

  • Ottawa & Local Audiences

    Residents interested in local entertainment featuring recognizable neighbourhoods, businesses, and community spaces.

  • Unscripted Entertainment Fans

    Audiences who enjoy comedy, competitions, group adventures, and personality-driven shows such as Taskmaster, The Amazing Race, and 2 Days & 1 Night.
